Complimentary Educational Guide · Salon Cyber Hygiene

Basic Cybersecurity & Client Data Privacy for Salons

In digital marketing, customer trust is inseparable from data security. Independent beauty salons collect sensitive personal information, including client contact details, medical or allergy notes for lash glues/tint tests, and booking card tokens.

As part of our commitment to ethical marketing education, Spotlight SA provides salon owners with four essential cyber-hygiene principles:

  • 1. Enforce Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Protect salon Instagram, Google, and email accounts from unauthorized hijackers by requiring an authenticator code.
  • 2. Use PCI-Compliant Booking Platforms: Never record credit card numbers in paper diaries or unsecured spreadsheets. Use recognized software (e.g. Fresha, Timely, Square) that encrypts payment tokens.
  • 3. Restrict Staff Account Access: Provide each staff practitioner with individual logins rather than sharing one master salon password.
  • 4. Clear Privacy Statement on Booking Forms: Reassure clients how their contact numbers and allergy patch-test records are securely managed in compliance with Australian privacy guidelines.

Note: This guide is provided for educational awareness. Spotlight SA provides digital marketing strategy and does not provide IT network or commercial cybersecurity audit services.
